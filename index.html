Art of Living in Synergy with Nature

Autonomous Living Ecosystems Habitat integrates shelter, water, food, energy and waste into one coherent, nature-first concept of independent living.

Let’s Talk

Nature First

We design habitats embedded in forest gardens – self-regulating ecosystems where humans regain a deep, respectful connection with their natural environment.

Mission

Our mission is to restore nature to its primary role in human life and help people rebuild a lost relationship with their natural habitat – a lush, biodiverse, vibrant forest garden.

The habitat is not just a building. It is a living node in a regenerative ecosystem, designed for food and energy independence.

Key Elements

  • Shelter: Modular, relocatable house up to 70 m²
  • Water: Rain harvesting, natural filtration
  • Food: Forest garden with all layers of vegetation
  • Energy: Solar-based electricity and passive heating
  • Waste: Plant-based wastewater treatment and composting
